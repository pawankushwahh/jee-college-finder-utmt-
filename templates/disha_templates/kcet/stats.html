Seat pools — Rest of Karnataka vs 371(j)

KEA publishes every category twice: once for the state-wide pool and once for the 371(j) Kalyana-Karnataka pool. The same category can close at a very different rank in each, so which pool you are eligible for matters as much as which category you hold.

About this data

KCET publishes a cut-off per round, so the range of ranks a programme actually admitted is observed rather than modelled — but only for programmes that appeared in more than one round.
